Concept: SPRINT — gap-on-linebacker. Terms: [[coaches/coach-one-pager|coach one-pager]].

Default is the handoff — Q only keeps and runs it himself if the coach signals it.

## Maroon

Maroon ZMO QB Sprint

![[sprint-maroon.png]]

- **Y**: Blocks the backside EMOL/DE, protects the edge away from the sprint.
- **BT / BG / C / FG / FT**: Gap-on-linebacker. Man in the gap away from the play, down block him. Man on you head up, reach and turn him. Nobody on you, climb and cut off a linebacker you can reach, usually the one further away.
- **Q**: Calls Z in motion pre-snap to get him into crack position on the DE, then keeps and sprints out around the perimeter behind H and F.
- **H**: Lead blocks around the outside for Q's keeper — first blocker out, kicks out the first threat in the alley.
- **F**: Lead blocks around the outside for Q's keeper, behind H — picks up the next threat that shows.
- **X**: Releases vertically upfield as a decoy, clearing out the middle. No block.
- **Z**: Goes in motion pre-snap to get in position, then crack blocks the DE.

## Gold

Gold QB Sprint

![[sprint-gold.png]]

- **Q**: No motion call needed — F is already aligned to crack. Keeps and sprints out around the perimeter behind H.
- **Y / BT / BG / C / FG / FT / H / X**: Unchanged from Maroon.
- **F**: Cracks the DE. No motion needed — he's already split out close enough to reach him from his starting alignment.
- **Z**: Stalk blocks the CB on him. No crossing motion, works from his own alignment.
