## Pre-Practice Stretching

### Sitting/static (legs & arms)
- Seated hamstring stretch (legs out, reach for toes)
- Butterfly stretch (soles together, knees out, lean forward)
- Seated groin/straddle stretch (legs wide, reach center then each side)
- Figure-4 / seated glute stretch (ankle over opposite knee, lean forward)
- Quad stretch (kneeling or side-lying, pull heel to butt)
- Seated cross-body shoulder stretch (pull arm across chest)
- Overhead triceps stretch (elbow behind head, pull with other hand)
- Neck stretch (ear to shoulder, each side)

### Dynamic/moving
- Butt kickers
- Karaoke (grapevine)
- Lateral shuffle
- Bounding (high-knee skips)
- Walking lunges
- High knees
- Leg swings (front-to-back and side-to-side, holding a fence/partner)
- A-skips
- Backpedal
- Straight-leg march/kicks

## Conditioning Week Drills (no contact, no pads)

Format: rotating stations — split into 4-5 groups of ~6, one coach per station, 30-45s work/rest, rotate every 3-4 min. Everyone's working the whole time, no waiting in lines. With a quarter-field footprint, run bigs and skill as two side-by-side station circuits.

1. Bear crawl shuttle — bear crawl to a cone and back, whole group goes together in waves.
2. Crab walk race — same footprint, works a different muscle group, good change of pace.
3. Partner-resisted sprint — pairs, one holds a towel/band around the other's waist and resists while they drive forward 10 yds, switch.
4. Mirror drill — pairs face off 5 yds apart, one leads lateral shuffle/backpedal/forward-shuffle, partner mirrors; switch leader every 20-30s.
5. Shuttle suicides (mini) — 5-10-5 yd markers, small group runs together on a whistle, everyone moving at once.
6. Up-down sprint — sprint 10 yds, up-down, sprint back, whole group in a line abreast (not queued single-file).
7. High-knee/butt-kicker ladder over cones — lay 8-10 cones in a row, group moves through together side by side (multiple parallel cone lines if space allows).
8. Broad jump / bound medley — standing broad jumps or lateral bounds down a 10-yd stretch, group abreast.
9. Tag games in a bounded grid — "sharks and minnows" or "everybody's it" tag inside a cone-marked box; whole team moves constantly, no contact, high fatigue, kids love it.
10. Star drill / 4-cone touch — cones in a plus-shape, sprint out-and-back to each in sequence; small groups run simultaneously on separate cone sets if space allows.
11. Plank-to-sprint — hold plank 20s, on whistle everyone sprints 10 yds and back, repeat.
12. Gasser relay — split each of the 2 position groups (bigs/skill) into 2-3 short relay teams so several races run in parallel, not one long line.
