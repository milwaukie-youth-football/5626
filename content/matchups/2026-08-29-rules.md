---
title: Jamboree Rules
---

Teaching/learning event, not a competitive one. No minimum-play requirement, no score kept.

## Format

- Field split at the 50: Scoreboard side and Snack Shack side, two scrimmages running at once.
- Each scrimmage: 45 minutes running time, ball starts at the 40-yard line, offense drives toward the end zone.
- Halftime of each scrimmage: teams switch offense/defense, 5 minutes allowed to swap.
- No kicking game, no timeouts.
- Each team gets a minimum of two scrimmages.
- Youth referees work the games — also using it to train newer officials.

## Carried over from the 2024 rules doc (not restated in the 2026 flyer, assume still in effect)

- No blitzing, stunting, or Sam/Mike/Will linebacker blitzes on defense. The strong-side linebacker can walk up in a 3-man front.
- After a score, ball goes back to the 40-yard line; offensive coach picks the hash. No first down by 4th down → ball resets to the 40 and starts over.
- Official time is the field scoreboard. No plays once it hits zero.

## Coaches on the field

- Two coaches per team allowed on the field during the scrimmage.
- Defensive coaches must stay at least 20 yards deeper than the deepest defender.
- All other coaches stay on the sideline, 3 yards back.

## Weigh-ins

- Each team weighs in once, 30 minutes before their scheduled scrimmage.
- TVYFL weight limits enforced, same as league play.
- Over the limit → horizontal tape stripe across front and back of the helmet, same as regular season.

## Filming

- One videographer per team allowed in the press box, must be 18+.
- Only allowed in the box while their team is actually playing.

## Facility / sportsmanship

- No pets in the stadium, on the track, or in the bleachers — every stadium used for youth football in the Portland area, all season.
- Concessions available; restrooms on the north side, under the stadium.
- Be supportive of all coaches and players from every program, not just ours.
- Reward effort regardless of win or loss. Don't raise complaints mid-game. Applaud good plays either way. Respect the officials. Ask "was it fun?" before "did you win?"
