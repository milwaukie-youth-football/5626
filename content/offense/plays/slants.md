Concept: SLANTS — pass block, ball out fast. Terms: [[coaches/coach-one-pager|coach one-pager]].

## Maroon

Maroon Slants

![[slants-maroon.png]]

- **Y**: Runs a 5-yard out, pulling up at the sidewalk instead of running out of bounds.
- **BT / BG / C / FG / FT**: Pass block. Man on you, punch and sit. Nobody on you, help the man next to you toward the middle.
- **Q**: 3-step drop, then throws off the read or scrambles. Reads Z to F; H is the safety valve.
- **H**: Releases as the checkdown, flaring out to the right.
- **F**: Slants from the backfield up and in, toward the middle. Keeps working across — never sits down, in any situation.
- **X**: Runs a seam up the middle that bends toward the sideline as it gets deep.
- **Z**: Slants from his split-out alignment up and in, toward the middle, mirroring F. Keeps working across — never sits down, in any situation.

## Gold

Gold Slants

![[slants-gold.png]]

Everyone does the exact same job as Maroon — Y, BT, BG, C, FG, FT, Q, H, X, and Z are unchanged.

- **F**: Shuffles out beyond the line X runs his seam on, then plants hard and breaks in for the slant. Same "keep working across" rule as Maroon.
