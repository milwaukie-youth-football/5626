Concept: DUO — `gap` → `down` → `buddy` → `backer`, in that order, every man, every time. Terms: [[coaches/coach-one-pager|coach one-pager]].

Default is the handoff to H — F's bubble is only thrown if the coach signals it.

## Maroon

Maroon H Duo / F Bubble

![[duo-maroon.png]]

Against a 4-man front this comes out to two clean double teams: BT+BG on the boundary DT, C+FG on the field DT. Y and FT aren't part of either double — they take the two EMOL/DEs on their outside shade instead.

- **Y**: Blocks the boundary EMOL/DE on his outside shade. Kicks him out, doesn't let him cross his face inside.
- **BT**: `buddy` — double teams the boundary DT with BG, then one of them climbs to the linebacker.
- **BG**: `buddy` — double teams the boundary DT with BT, then one of them climbs.
- **C**: `buddy` — double teams the field DT with FG, then one of them climbs.
- **FG**: `buddy` — double teams the field DT with C, then one of them climbs.
- **FT**: Blocks the field EMOL/DE on his outside shade. Kicks him out.
- **Q**: Straight handoff to H. No fake, no keep.
- **H**: Takes the handoff, presses downhill, picks his crease off whichever double team pushes cleanest — Duo's a read, not a fixed hole.
- **F**: Bubbles out to the perimeter, giving Q a quick pass option off the run look.
- **X**: Releases downfield, stalk blocks his man.
- **Z**: Releases downfield, stalk blocks his man.

## Gold

Gold H Duo / F Bubble

![[duo-gold.png]]

Everyone does the exact same job as Maroon — Y, BT, BG, C, FG, FT, Q, H, X, and Z are unchanged. F's job doesn't even change here, just his starting point.

- **F**: Same bubble route as Maroon, giving Q the same quick pass option — just a shorter path to it since he's already split out near X instead of starting in the backfield.
