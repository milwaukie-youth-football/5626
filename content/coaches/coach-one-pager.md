## Positions — six letters, that's the whole list

| Letter | Who | Where |
|---|---|---|
| **Q** | Quarterback | Under center / gun |
| **H** | Running back | Backfield |
| **F** | Slot / back | Off the line |
| **Y** | Tight end | **On the line**, end man |
| **X** | Wide receiver | **On the line** |
| **Z** | Wide receiver | **Off the line** |

**Base formation: GG4.** Two backs, Y attached, X and Z twins.
**Change-up: NUB TRIPS.** One back, Y alone backside, three receivers to the field.

**Under 130 lbs required:** Q, H, F, Y, X, Z. All six. Y counts because he is the end man on the line.
Over 130 = fluorescent stripe on the helmet, interior line and defense only.

## The ten blocking words

Kids learn these words, not schemes.

| Word | What the kid does |
|---|---|
| `gap` | Block the man in the gap to my playside. First thing I look for. |
| `down` | No gap threat. Block the man inside me, on the next guy over. |
| `buddy` | Double team with the man next to me, then one of us climbs. |
| `backer` | Nobody down to block. Climb to the linebacker. |
| `pin` | I'm covered playside. Block him down and in. Don't let him cross my face. |
| `pull` | I'm uncovered. Leave, run flat, get outside. |
| `kick` | First puller. Blow the outside man OUT. |
| `wrap` | Second puller. Turn up inside the kick, hit the first wrong color. |
| `scoop` | Backside. Cut him off, don't let him chase. |
| `trap` | Leave the man outside me alone. Someone is coming to kick him. |
| `reach` | Man is head up on me. Get to his outside and turn him. |

---

## The one rule that survives every front

**Covered = pin. Uncovered = pull.**

Works against 4-4, 5-3 and 6-2 without a new install. We will see all three.
**First puller kicks. Second puller wraps.** Every time.

## The five concepts — OL rule for each

| Concept | OL rule |
|---|---|
| **TRAP** | Playside: `gap` `down`. Playside guard `trap` — leave him. Backside guard `pull` and `kick`. Center `scoop`. |
| **DUO** | `gap` → `down` → `buddy` → `backer`. In that order, every man, every time. |
| **SWEEP** | Pin-and-pull. Covered `pin`, uncovered `pull`. First puller `kick`, second `wrap`. Backside `scoop`. |
| **SPRINT** | Gap-on-linebacker. Man in the gap away from the run? `down`. Man on you, head up? `reach`, turn him. Nobody? Climb and cut off a linebacker you can reach, usually one further away than you. |
| **SLANTS** | Pass block. Man on you, punch and sit. Nobody on you? Help the man next to you, toward the middle. Ball is out fast. |

### SWEEP: the two pullers

Same two jobs as buck sweep. What changes is **who** does them. Covered/uncovered decides who pulls, so it might be both guards, a guard and the playside tackle, or the tackle and center. The jobs never change.

| | Job | Path |
|---|---|---|
| **First puller** (closest to playside, out first) | `kick` — blow out the first defender outside the last pin block | Flat and tight, right off the down blocks |
| **Second puller** (next one inside, out second) | `wrap` — turn up inside the kick, block the first wrong color | One step deeper, behind the first puller |

Kid version: **"First one out kicks. Second one out goes around him and kills the first different jersey."**

Two things we are NOT teaching this year:
- **No log.** If the kick target wrong-arms and comes upfield inside, the puller still kicks. The back cuts up behind him. A kick-or-log decision in space is too much for a 10-year-old pulling at full speed.
- **Fixed tracks, always.** First puller flat and tight, second puller one step deeper. Same two paths every time, whoever is pulling. This is how we keep them from running into each other.

---

**OL learns five things.** Skill learns ten pictures, because two backs vs one back changes the mesh and the bubble source.

**Bubbles:** GG4 trap and duo, F bubbles. GG4 sweep, F carries and X bubbles. TRIPS trap, Q keeps. TRIPS duo and sweep, F bubbles.

The bubble is legal with the line firing out. A pass caught behind the line never crosses the neutral zone, so nobody is illegally downfield.

## Defense — 5-3 base man

- Five down, three linebackers, two corners, one safety.
- **Count the receivers to your side: #1, #2, #3.** You have your number. That's your man.
- **Motion: run with him.** He moves, you move. No talking, no switching.
- **S stays in the middle.** S never chases motion. S is the last guy. His job is to tackle whoever gets through.

## Week 1: conditioning only, no pads, helmets allowed

Ten hours, league rule. Do not try to install football. Do these:

- **Weigh every kid.** Determines the stripe list and who can play the six skill spots.
- **Time everybody.** Find out who is fast.
- Stance: 2-point athletic, 3-point.
- Alignment: where do I stand in GG4, where in NUB TRIPS.
- Huddle, break, sprint to the ball, get set.
- **Cadence.** Getting off on the snap beats teams at this level and costs zero contact minutes.
- Ball security, four points.

## Do not say these words to the kids

MDM, MOR, EMOL, the box, the sidewalk, the alley, inside zone, outside zone, half-slide.
Coach words. They cost attention we don't have.

---

## What they will not learn this season

Don't spend hours on it:

- Reading a front pre-snap and adjusting.
- Man coverage across motion (which is why the rule is just "run with him").
- Any concept with a second read.

---

## Standing rules

- **Ten plays per kid, from scrimmage.** No special teams at 5/6, so all ten come from real snaps.
- All tens done **before the 4th quarter.** Never more than one or two ten-play kids on the field together.
- Contact cap: **3.5 hrs/week before Labor Day, 90 min/week after.** Tackling competes with everything.
- After Labor Day practice drops to **6 hrs/week**, three sessions. Plan the install to finish before that.
