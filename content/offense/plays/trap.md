Concept: TRAP — playside guard `trap`, backside guard `pull` and `kick`, center `scoop`. Terms: [[coaches/coach-one-pager|coach one-pager]].

Default is the handoff to H — F's bubble (Maroon) and Q's keep (Gold) only happen if the coach signals it.

### Field Right

![[trap-maroon-right.png]]

- **Y**: Seals the edge, blocking the playside EMOL/DE.
- **BT**: Playside — climbs immediately to the playside linebacker, cutting him off.
- **BG**: Playside — climbs immediately to the backside linebacker, cutting him off.
- **C**: Down blocks the DT nearest FG, on the backside.
- **FG**: Pulls — steps back at a 45° angle, runs down the line behind C's down block, and traps the playside DT. Left shoulder if pulling left, right shoulder if pulling right.
- **FT**: Doesn't waste time on the DE — climbs to a linebacker or the safety instead.
- **Q**: Hands off to H.
- **H**: Runs downhill, right up the gut, off FG's trap block.
- **F**: Backside bubble — only thrown if the coach signals it.
- **X**: Releases vertically as a decoy, clearing out the middle. No block.
- **Z**: Stalk blocks the CB on him.

![[trap-gold-left.png]]

### Field Right

Everyone does the exact same job as Maroon — Y, BT, BG, C, FG, FT, H, X, and Z are unchanged.

- **Q**: Hands off to H by default — keeps it himself on the backside only if the coach signals it.
- **F**: No bubble to throw in Gold — stalk blocks his man instead.
