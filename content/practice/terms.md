---
title: 5/6 Terms
---

Terms we want the kids to know.

- `Sideline`: the boundary line running the length of the field on each side; the field is out of bounds past it.
- `Goal Line`: the line at each end of the field a team must cross to score a touchdown.
- `Hashes`: the two short marks running down the middle of the field, one set every yard, that mark where the ball is placed for the next play when the previous play ended between the hashes and the sideline.
- `The Numbers`: the yard-marker numbers painted on the field showing distance from the nearest goal line.
- `Top of the Numbers`: the edge of the yard-marker numbers closest to the middle of the field (closest to the hashes).
- `Bottom of the Numbers`: the edge of the yard-marker numbers closest to the sideline.
- `Boundary`: the sideline and the space just outside it; "out of bounds."
- `Field`: the full playing surface, sideline to sideline, goal line to goal line.
- `Line of Scrimmage` (`LOS`): the invisible line across the field, at the ball, that separates offense and defense before the snap.
- `On the Ball`: a player lined up with their body touching or right on the line of scrimmage.
- `Off the Ball`: a player lined up a step or more behind the line of scrimmage, not touching it.
- `Eligible Receiver on the LOS`: a player on the line of scrimmage who is allowed to catch a pass (for example, the players at each end of the offensive line, not the interior linemen).
- `EMOL` (End Man on the LOS): the last player on the line of scrimmage on either side; usually the defensive end, but can be an outside linebacker rolled up to the line.
- `MDM` (Most Dangerous Man): the defender in the best position to disrupt the offensive play or make the tackle.
