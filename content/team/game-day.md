## Arrival

Meet **1 hour 15 minutes before kickoff**, in pads, cleats, and helmet + mouthpiece on.

## What players need to bring

- **White game jersey** — not the maroon practice jersey
- Mouthguard (attach a strap to the helmet so it doesn't get left behind)
- Cleats — **molded only, no metal spikes** (league rule)
- Game pants
- Pads — shoulder pads, girdle (hip/tail/knee pads)
- Helmet
- Water bottle, labeled with name

## Other reminders

- **No jewelry** — strictly prohibited, no exceptions (league rule)
- Athletic supporter/cup
- Sunscreen and layers for the weather
- Snack/drink for after

See [[coaches/coach-prep-checklist|coach prep checklist]] and [[matchups/jamboree-rules|Jamboree Rules]] for the full league equipment and conduct rules.
