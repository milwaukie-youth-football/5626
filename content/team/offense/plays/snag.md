Concept: SNAG — quick-game pass, same as Stick with X and Z swapped. Terms: [[coaches/coach-one-pager|coach one-pager]].

## Maroon

Maroon Snag

### Field Right

![[snag-maroon-right.png]]

- **Y**: Runs a 5-yard out, pulling up at the sidewalk instead of running out of bounds.
- **BT / BG / C / FG / FT**: Pass block. Man on you, punch and sit. Nobody on you, help the man next to you toward the middle.
- **Q**: Drops and reads X to Z to F, H as the outlet.
- **H**: Slower swing route, releasing behind where X lined up. Don't get to the flat too fast and end up on top of F.
- **F**: Sprints to the flat, stops at the sideline.
- **X**: 7-8 yard corner — keep going at 45 degrees to the sideline, no stop.
- **Z**: Hitch (snag) to 6 along X's route, then comes back to 5 — settle behind where the overhang OLB will probably line up.

Note: this play can also be called max protect, keeping Y and H in to pass block instead of running their routes.

Everyone does the exact same job as Maroon — Y, BT, BG, C, FG, FT, Q, H, X, and Z are unchanged.

- **F**: Same route as Maroon, sprint to the flat and stop at the sideline — just a shorter path since he's already split out near X instead of starting in the backfield.
