## 3 Coach Requirements (due 8/9)
1. **MYF registration + background check** — register, then complete background check via follow-up email.
2. **USA Football Youth Tackle Certification**
   - Create/login USA Football account → Organizations → associate with "Milwaukie Youth Football And Cheer-Tvyfl"
   - Learning Center → pick cert → Get Certified → promo code:
     - Flag: `MILWAUKIEFLAG26`
     - Tackle: `MILWAUKIETACKLE26`
   - Questions: support@usafootball.com
3. **CPR/First Aid certification**
   - Free options: firstaidforfree.com or nationalcprfoundation.com
   - Send cert to: **mustang.fc.registrar@gmail.com**
