Concept: SWEEP — pin-and-pull. Covered `pin`, uncovered `pull`. First puller `kick`, second `wrap`. Backside `scoop`. Terms: [[coaches/coach-one-pager|coach one-pager]].

### Field Right

![[sweep-maroon-right.png]]

- **Y**: Exception to the OL rule — if he can reach block the EMOL with an outside shade, he does that instead. If not, the same pull rule applies to him too.
- **BT / BG / C / FG / FT**: Check your backside gap. Someone there? Down block him. Nobody there? You pull. First one out kicks the first different-colored jersey outside. Second one out wraps around him and cuts off whatever pursuit shows next.
- **Q**: Hands off to F.
- **H**: Leads the sweep, kicks out the first threat outside once he clears the box.
- **F**: Takes the handoff and sweeps outside, following the pullers' kick and wrap.
- **X**: Bubbles out to the perimeter, giving Q a quick pass option off the run look.
- **Z**: Stalk blocks the CB on him.

## Gold

Gold H Sweep F Bubble

### Field Left

![[sweep-gold-left.png]]

### Field Right

![[sweep-gold-right.png]]

Everyone does the exact same job as Maroon on the OL — Y and BT, BG, C, FG, FT are unchanged. Z is also unchanged.

- **Q**: Hands off to H (no F in the backfield to hand off to).
- **H**: Takes the handoff and sweeps outside, following the pullers' kick and wrap.
- **F**: Bubbles out to the perimeter from his split-out alignment, giving Q the same quick pass option — just a shorter path since he's already out there.
- **X**: Stalk blocks the perimeter defender on him, since F has taken over the bubble.

Default is the handoff to the sweep runner (F in Maroon, H in Gold) — the backside bubble is only thrown if the coach signals it.

Note: this could be simplified to always run F Bubble on the backside, in both Maroon and Gold, and always hand it off to H — removing the carrier swap between F and H.
