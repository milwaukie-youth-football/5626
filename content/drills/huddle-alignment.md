---
title: Huddle & Alignment
---

Teaching the huddle call: formation + wide side. No contact.

## Calls

- **Formation** — [[Maroon]] (2 backs in the backfield) or [[Gold]] (3 WRs wide)
- **Wide** — `Left` or `Right`. Sets which side the 2/3 WRs line up on.
- **Y (TE)** lines up opposite the Wide call, every time.

## Static (10 reps)

Huddle up, coach makes the call, players walk into alignment and hold. Check every kid before resetting.

## Dynamic (10 reps)

Same call, but break the huddle and sprint to alignment, get set like it's a real snap.

20 reps total (10 static + 10 dynamic).
